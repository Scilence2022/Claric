Claric

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-29 · Applies to Claric 1.0.0 and later

Claric is an AI editing assistant for Microsoft Word. This policy explains exactly what data moves, where it goes, and what stays on your machine. The short version: Claric has no built-in analytics collection. Document content, selected images and attachments may be sent to your configured chat, image or MCP providers, directly or through your operator's relay. Settings and chat history are stored locally in the add-in's browser storage.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Claric is distributed as a self-hosted add-in. The person or organization operating the Claric web service you connect to (your company or your chosen hosting provider) is the operator; Claric's developer does not operate any collection infrastructure. Questions about a specific deployment go to that operator; questions about the software itself go to GitHub Issues.

2. What leaves your machine

  • Document content you ask about. When you request an edit, review, summary, or answer, relevant selections, document sections, comments and tracked changes are sent to your configured AI endpoint.
  • Images, attachments and external tools. Selected images, extracted attachment text, image-generation prompts and MCP tool arguments may go to separately configured services. Startup and connection tests also contact configured endpoints; image connection tests may generate a billable image.
  • Every configured route matters. A local chat model does not make a cloud image provider, remote MCP server or relay local. Information sent to a provider is subject to its privacy and retention terms. Review all routes before processing confidential documents.

3. Local storage and credentials

  • Your API keys are stored in the add-in's browser local storage and sent to configured endpoints, through your operator's proxy when enabled. These endpoints and relays receive your credentials; verify them before entering a key. Claric does not encrypt browser storage, and same-origin scripts can access it.
  • Your settings, saved prompts, and chat history live in local storage on your machine and can be erased at any time (browser site-data controls, or in-app via the history panel).
  • No telemetry. Claric makes no analytics calls, runs no tracking scripts, and reports no usage statistics.

4. Data handling in organizations

If Claric was deployed by your organization, the operator controls the hosting domain, which AI providers are proxied, and whether requests are logged at their infrastructure. Your organization's own privacy notice applies on top of this one. Users in regulated industries (legal, finance, healthcare) should prefer locally hosted models for confidential material.

5. Your choices and controls

  • Review chat, image and MCP routes independently before sending content. A local model alone does not guarantee confidentiality of all operations.
  • Edits are staged by default. Enabling Auto-apply explicitly authorizes automatic document changes without a separate click on each proposal. Stopping an operation does not undo changes already submitted to Word.
  • Delete saved sessions and prompts from the add-in UI; clearing browser site data removes locally stored settings and keys. Local deletion does not delete data already retained by a provider or relay.

6. Children

Claric is a professional productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction).

7. Changes to this policy

Material changes will be reflected in the updated policy published at this URL and noted in the add-in's release notes.
