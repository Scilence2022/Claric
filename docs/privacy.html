Claric

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-29 · Applies to Claric 1.0.0 and later

Claric is an AI editing assistant for Microsoft Word. This policy explains exactly what data moves, where it goes, and what stays on your machine. The short version: Claric itself has no servers and collects nothing — your document text goes only to the AI endpoint you configure, and everything else lives in your browser's local storage on your own machine.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Claric is distributed as a self-hosted add-in. The person or organization operating the Claric web service you connect to (your company or your chosen hosting provider) is the operator; Claric's developer does not operate any collection infrastructure. Questions about a specific deployment go to that operator; questions about the software itself go to GitHub Issues.

2. What leaves your machine — and only when you send a request

  • Document content you ask about. When you request an edit, review, summary, or answer, the relevant text — your selection, the document sections being processed, document comments, or tracked changes — is sent to the AI endpoint you configured in Settings (for example, your local Ollama server, or a cloud provider such as DeepSeek, Zhipu GLM, Moonshot Kimi, or MiniMax).
  • Where it goes is up to you. If you configure a local model, the document text never leaves your computer. If you configure a cloud provider, the text goes to that provider and is subject to their privacy terms — review them before sending confidential documents.

3. What never leaves your machine

  • Your API keys are stored in the add-in's browser local storage and are sent only to the endpoint you configured (or, when same-origin proxying is enabled by your operator, only through that proxy to the provider you chose). They are never sent to Claric's developers or any third party.
  • Your settings, saved prompts, and chat history live in local storage on your machine and can be erased at any time (browser site-data controls, or in-app via the history panel).
  • No telemetry. Claric makes no analytics calls, runs no tracking scripts, and reports no usage statistics.

4. Data handling in organizations

If Claric was deployed by your organization, the operator controls the hosting domain, which AI providers are proxied, and whether requests are logged at their infrastructure. Your organization's own privacy notice applies on top of this one. Users in regulated industries (legal, finance, healthcare) should prefer locally hosted models for confidential material.

5. Your choices and controls

  • Choose where your text goes — local model for full confidentiality, or a cloud provider you trust.
  • Every AI-proposed edit is staged for your review; nothing is written into your document until you apply it.
  • Delete saved sessions and prompts from the add-in UI at any time; clearing browser site data removes all locally stored settings and keys.

6. Children

Claric is a professional productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction).

7. Changes to this policy

Material changes will be reflected in the updated policy published at this URL and noted in the add-in's release notes.
